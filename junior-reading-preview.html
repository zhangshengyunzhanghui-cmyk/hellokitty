READING PASSAGE

Traveling is fun, and technology can help us travel in a more responsible way. In many places, visitors are learning how small actions can protect the environment.

For example, a nature park can use digital tickets to reduce paper waste. Visitors can also learn about local plants through their phones. These tools make it easier to enjoy nature without damaging it.

Technology is useful, but people still need to make good choices. By following the rules and respecting the places they visit, travelers can help keep these places beautiful for the future.
